    About Us

    Flowers are a great way to show appreciation to the people we love, or to celebrate life's moments. It's a beautiful way to spread joy by bringing together earth's lovely blooms.

    How We Started

    What started as one gift-giver's passion in Portland has grown into something bigger: a commitment to healthier, longer-lasting flowers — grown and sourced with the planet in mind. At FloralBay, eco-friendliness isn't an afterthought; it's built into everything we do, from how we source to how we deliver.

    How We Grow

    Our flowers are grown in organic, nutrient-rich soil — a careful 40:40:20 blend of sand, silt, and clay — designed for ideal drainage, aeration, and moisture retention. Better soil means healthier plants, and healthier plants mean flowers with a longer vase life.

    How We Arrange

    Whether we're arranging flowers for your event or delivering them to your door, we handle every step with care — so your flowers arrive fresh and stay beautiful for as long as possible.
